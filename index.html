Car Shortlist privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Applies to Car Shortlist v1.2

Your saved-car Garage is stored in your Chrome profile. TitanForge Digital does not operate a server that receives your Garage. Optional Pro purchases use ExtensionPay and Stripe. Viewing saved photographs also contacts the relevant image host.

1. Who provides Car Shortlist

Car Shortlist is provided by TitanForge Digital, the developer using the GitHub account titanforge-digital. This policy describes the Chrome extension and this policy website. For privacy questions, use the privacy-policy repository's contact and issue page. GitHub issues are public: do not post payment information, email login links, credentials, private advert links or Garage backups. You can request a private contact route without including those details.

2. Information stored in your Garage

When you choose to save or update a car, the extension reads available information from that advert, including its title, URL, price, mileage, year, make/model, trim/specification, engine size, fuel, transmission, registration plate where disclosed, dealer name, seller location, availability and photograph URL. Some sites provide only a subset. Unknown details can be completed manually.

The extension also stores information you enter or select: notes, favourites, buying budget and must-have features, review checkboxes, progress, running-cost assumptions, annual mileage, saved comparison order, and dated price observations. Some view controls and comparison selections exist only for the current dashboard session.

These Garage records use chrome.storage.local, within the current Chrome profile. They are not uploaded to TitanForge Digital or ExtensionPay, and Car Shortlist does not provide Garage cloud sync. Notes and exported backups are not encrypted by the extension. Avoid entering sensitive personal information.

3. Website access and when pages are read

On supported Auto Trader, eBay, Carwow, CarGurus, MOTORS and Cazoo sites, a bundled content script displays the save button and checks the page URL against your saved listings. Vehicle extraction happens after your save action. Other dealer websites can be saved using the toolbar/right-click fallback. You can optionally grant access to a particular HTTPS dealer site to enable its button; revoke this in Chrome's extension settings.

Choosing “Update from open adverts” matches tab URLs on permitted sites to saved cars and reads up to 20 matching open adverts per action. The extension does not run unattended vehicle-price monitoring, automatically reload adverts, or collect your general browsing history. Its permissions support storage, user-invoked page reading, bundled script injection and right-click commands. ExtensionPay website access supports purchase confirmation and restoration.

The code is designed to capture vehicle details, not passwords, cookies, payment fields or private messages. Advert URLs can themselves contain quote identifiers or private tokens, particularly on Carwow. Those URLs are retained in your Garage and backups, so keep them private.

4. Photographs and external websites

The extension stores photograph links rather than permanent image copies. Displaying a photograph makes an HTTPS request to its marketplace, dealer or image-hosting service. That service receives your IP address, requested image URL and normal connection information. Image elements use a no-referrer policy. Saved notes and the rest of your Garage are not attached to these requests. Photographs may expire or be unavailable offline.

Clicking “Open listing” opens the advertiser's website normally. Those websites and image services apply their own privacy policies.

5. Optional Pro purchase and restoration

Free includes the existing tools and up to five saved cars. Pro provides unlimited saved cars for a £4.99 one-off lifetime payment. No recurring subscription is implemented in v1.2.

The locally bundled ExtensionPay library contacts extensionpay.com to obtain plan information, open checkout or purchase sign-in, and verify paid status. It creates a purchase/installation credential for payment or restoration and stores that credential, an installation timestamp and returned account information such as paid status, purchase date, plan and email when supplied by ExtensionPay.

Unlike Garage records, these ExtensionPay values use Chrome sync storage, falling back to local storage if sync is unavailable. Chrome may sync them between browsers according to your Google/Chrome settings. This is purchase-related storage, not syncing your saved cars. Backups of the Garage do not contain those purchase credentials.

ExtensionPay and its payment provider Stripe handle checkout and account recovery. They may process your email, transaction information, payment details and normal network/security information. Car Shortlist does not read or store your full card number or security code. TitanForge Digital may have access to purchase/account information through the providers' merchant tools for fulfilment, support, refunds and required business records; this does not give access to your local Garage.

“Restore Purchase / Sign In” uses ExtensionPay's email-based recovery. Status checks occur on relevant use and confirmation events; the payment library may briefly retry verification after purchase. They do not upload saved-car records. See ExtensionPay's privacy information, Stripe's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy for their processing, international transfers, safeguards and retention.

6. Purposes and data use

Local records are used to deliver the saving, comparison, notes, backup and running-cost tools you request. Purchase/account information is used to fulfil and verify Pro access, restore purchases and handle support or refunds. Where TitanForge Digital handles personal information, relevant grounds are performing the purchase contract, legitimate interests in providing support and preventing misuse, and complying with applicable legal record-keeping requirements.

Version 1.2 contains no TitanForge Digital analytics, advertising, affiliate tracking, sale of personal data, or remote Garage database. Local comparison/cost calculations do not contact vehicle-data, insurance, postcode or routing services. This policy describes current code, not proposed roadmap features.

7. Retention, export and deletion

Garage records remain in the Chrome profile until you edit/delete them, clear the Garage or remove the extension/profile. Individual price histories are limited to 50 observations per car. Use Export backup to obtain a JSON copy. Exported files and printed/PDF copies remain wherever you save them; deleting the Garage or uninstalling does not delete those copies.

“Delete all local data” clears the Garage, not your purchase or the payment library's separate sync/local values. Removing the extension does not erase payment-provider account or transaction records. Contact the relevant provider and TitanForge Digital about such records; purchase verification may be needed. Payment/security and legally required business-record retention are separate from your local Garage. Information shared for support should be limited to what is needed to resolve the request and any applicable record-keeping obligations.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use Free without buying Pro, edit or delete local cars, export a backup, revoke website access or uninstall. TitanForge Digital cannot recover Garage data it never receives. Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing of personal information held by TitanForge Digital or the relevant provider. Some requests are subject to identity checks and legal exceptions. Use the contact route above for privacy questions; do not post personal records publicly. UK users can raise concerns with the Information Commissioner's Office.

9. This privacy-policy website

This is a static GitHub Pages website with no analytics, forms, external fonts, embedded images or scripts added by TitanForge Digital. GitHub hosts it and records visitors' IP addresses for security. GitHub's services, including repository issues, have their own data handling under the GitHub privacy statement. Opening external links is your choice.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when relevant functionality or data handling changes. The date and applicable extension version above identify this notice.
